## 1. Visual Philosophy & Identity

This Short continues the **dark technical aesthetic** of AI News Factory while pivoting from aerospace engineering into **computational molecular biology**.

### Core Priorities:
1. **Immediate Hook (0.0s – 2.0s):** Break viewer expectations instantly. Transition from digital computational code into physical viral biology.
2. **Scientific Precision:** Avoid cartoon biology, fantasy mutant DNA, or generic chatbots. Present realistic structural biology (icosahedral phages, globular enzymatic clefts, CRISPR-like repeating arrays).
3. **Strict Boundary Visualization:** Explicitly prevent viewers from inferring clinical gene editing or human gene therapy through prominent technical guardrail badges.
4. **Information Density & Pace:** Deliver a new visual event or camera progression every 3.0 to 5.5 seconds.
